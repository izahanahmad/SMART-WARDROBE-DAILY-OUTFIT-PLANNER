# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Busy working professionals, students, fashion-conscious individuals, and daily commuters who manage a collection of clothes and need to decide what to wear each day.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Individuals spend unnecessary time and effort every morning trying to pair clothes, often facing decision fatigue, underutilizing garments hidden in their closets, or choosing outfits unsuitable for the day's weather, schedule, or occasion.

## Why does it matter?
This daily friction leads to wasted morning time, increased stress, financial inefficiency due to underused clothing, and poor outfit choices that can impact daily confidence and comfort.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Busy professionals and daily commuters frequently suffer from decision fatigue and wasted time when choosing daily outfits from their wardrobes. Without an efficient way to track and pair clothes, many garments remain underutilized while users struggle to coordinate outfits suited for specific weather conditions or events. This daily inefficiency increases morning stress and prevents individuals from making optimal, confident styling choices. Smart Wardrobe Daily Outfit Planner addresses this by providing an automated, organized daily clothing selection tool to streamline morning routines.

## Users & Stakeholder?
Main users: Individuals needing assistance with managing their clothing collection and planning daily outfits.
Other stakeholders: Course evaluators, project assessors, and myself as the developer.

## Scope
Listing wardrobe items categorized by clothing type, color, and occasion.   
Filtering clothing items by category and style.   
Planning and scheduling daily outfit combinations.   

## Out of scope
Direct online shopping or buying clothes through external stores.
Assigning personal stylists or professional fashion consultants to users.
Logging in or storing user banking and payment data.
